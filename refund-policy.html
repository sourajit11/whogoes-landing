Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

WhoGoes (whogoes.co and app.whogoes.co) is operated by AVRPIX Solutions Pvt Ltd, Pune, India. This policy covers all payments made to WhoGoes.

2. Try before you buy

Every new account receives free trial credits. You can use them to unlock real contacts and check the quality of our data before you pay anything. We encourage you to use the trial fully before buying a credit pack.

3. What you buy and how it is delivered

  • WhoGoes sells one-time credit packs. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.
  • Credits are a digital product. They are added to your account instantly after a successful payment. Nothing is shipped.
  • Credits are used to unlock contact details in the WhoGoes app. Unlocked contacts stay available in your account and can be exported.
  • Credits do not expire.

4. Refunds

Because you can try WhoGoes for free first, and because credits are delivered instantly, all credit pack purchases are final and non-refundable.

We will refund a payment in full in these cases:

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase (duplicate charge)
  • You were charged but the credits were not added to your account because of a technical failure on our side, and we cannot add them

If you think either applies, email hello@whogoes.co within 30 days of the payment with the email address on your account and the payment date or payment ID. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through our payment processor, Razorpay. They usually reach your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. Any credits from a refunded payment are removed from your account.

6. Cancellation

There are no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel. You will never be charged again unless you choose to buy another credit pack. You can close your account at any time by emailing hello@whogoes.co. Unused credits are not refundable when an account is closed.

7. Contact

Questions about a payment? Email us at hello@whogoes.co.
