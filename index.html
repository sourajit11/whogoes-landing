Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

There are two ways to unlock. Unlock without filters (the whole list or just a partial slice) and it is 1 credit per contact with every verified email included free. Or filter to your ICP first: 1 credit unlocks each matching contact's identity (name, job title, company, LinkedIn profile and the source post), and revealing that contact's verified work email is an optional +1 credit, only on contacts that have one. You get 20 free credits on signup, and packs run from $29 for 200 credits up to $149 for 2,000. Credits never expire.

Can I filter the list before spending credits?

Yes. Filter any event's attendee list by seniority, function, industry, company size, country, event role (organizer, sponsor, exhibitor, attendee), and speaker status, or search by job title or company. You see how many contacts match and how many have a verified email before paying, then unlock only the matches.

What does the free preview include?

Every event page shows 5 real contacts for free, with no sign-up required. You can see names, titles, companies, and LinkedIn post URLs to verify data quality before creating an account.

How many contacts are available per event?

Varies by event size, shown on each event page. Ranges from a few hundred to 5,000+. Active events keep growing as new people post about attending.
